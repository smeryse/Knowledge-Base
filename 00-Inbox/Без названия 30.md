- **Введение темы (Present Simple):** "Nowadays, [тема/проблема] is a very common issue. Many people face this problem every day." _(Например: Nowadays, e-waste is a very common issue. Many people face this problem every day.)_
- **Приведение примера (Present Simple / can):** "For example, people can [действие] instead of [действие + ing]." _(Например: For example, people can buy a new smartphone every year instead of repairing the old one.)_
- **Личный опыт (Past Simple):** "As for me, I realized that [факт]. That is why I decided to [действие]." _(Например: As for me, I realized that I had too many old gadgets. That is why I decided to recycle them.)_
- **Вывод (Future Simple / believe):** "In conclusion, I believe that we will [действие] in the future. However, if we [условие], we will solve this problem."
