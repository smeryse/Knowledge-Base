### Блок 1. Профессиональные и IT-темы

###### **1. The information age and the problem**
Nowadays, the information age is a very important period, but it has a big problem: information ==overload==. Many people face this problem every day. 
For example, we ==receive== thousands of emails, messages, and news notifications, and it is hard to find ==reliable== information. 
As for me, I ==realized== that I spent too much time reading useless news. That is why I decided to limit my screen time. 
In ==conclusion==, I believe that information will become even more ==overwhelming== in the future. However, if we learn to filter it, we will save our time and mental health.

###### **2. Talk about e-crime**
Nowadays e-crime is a very ==common issue==. Many people face this problem every day. 
For example, scammers can steal money from trusting people. They call them, ==pretend== to be bank workers, and trick them. 
As for my family, these scammers almost tricked my parents. They called my mom and asked for her card details, but she ==hung up==. 
In conclusion, I believe that e-crime will become more dangerous in the future. However, if we are careful and don't share our personal data, we will stay safe.

###### **3. Talk about e-waste problem**
Nowadays, e-waste is a very common issue. Many people face this problem every day because we buy too many gadgets. 
For example, people can buy a new smartphone every year ==instead== of repairing the old one, which creates huge ==rubbish dumps==. 
As for me, I realized that I had too many old cables and broken headphones in my ==dorm==. That is why I decided to take them to a special recycling point. 
In ==conclusion==, I believe that e-waste will become a global ==disaster== in the future. However, if we recycle our electronics, we will protect the ==environment==.

###### **4. Talk about addiction to the Internet**
Nowadays, addiction to the Internet is a very common issue. Many people face this problem every day. 
For example, people can spend hours scrolling through and sharing videos on TikTok or YouTube Shorts ==instead== of doing something useful. 
As for me, I realized that I spend around six or seven hours a day on my phone. That is why I decided to spend my time more productively and do something interesting in real life. 
In conclusion, I believe that internet addiction will become a bigger problem in the future. ==However==, if we control our screen time, we will live a better life.

###### **5. Talk about buying a new computer**
==Nowadays==, buying a new computer is a very important decision for a student or a programmer. Many people face the problem of choosing the right model. 
For example, you can choose a powerful gaming laptop, but it is very heavy, or you can buy a lightweight ultrabook for studying. As for me, I realized that my old gaming laptop was **too heavy and noisy**. That is why I decided to sell it and buy a **simple, lightweight ultrabook for studying and everyday tasks**. In conclusion, I believe that ultrabooks will become even more popular in the future. However, if we choose the right model, we will be able to work comfortably anywhere
###### **6. Famous ==inventors==**
Nowadays, we use the inventions of famous people every day, and it is important to remember them. Many people face the problem of not knowing the history of technology.
For example, Linus Torvalds created the Linux operating system, and Richard Stallman started the free software movement. **Thanks to them, all modern smart devices work today. This is because most smartphones run on Android, and Android is actually a fork of Linux.** So, their code is inside billions of devices around the world.
As for me, I use the Ubuntu operating system every day for my studies. I realized that these programmers changed our lives just as much as the inventors of physical machines.
In conclusion, I believe that open-source technology will become even more important in the future. However, if we don't study history, we will forget how our modern IT world was created
